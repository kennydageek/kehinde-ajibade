# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Hiring teams, engineering managers, recruiters, founders, and contract clients evaluating Kehinde Ajibade for senior frontend engineering roles and project work. They need to quickly understand his production experience, technical leadership, architectural range, reliability, and fit for teams building AI, FinTech, SaaS, and PropTech products.

## Product Purpose

This portfolio presents Kehinde as a senior frontend engineer who architects scalable, performant applications across React, Next.js, Vue.js, TypeScript, reusable component systems, complex API integration, and testing. Success means visitors leave confident in his ability to lead serious frontend work while collaborating effectively across product teams.

## Brand Personality

Cool, chill, creative, innovative, eager to learn, and professional. The voice should feel confident without being stiff: technically credible, visually expressive, easy to read, and grounded in real work.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic portfolio templates, overused "genericfolioport" layouts, crypto-dashboard styling, overly corporate resume pages, and AI-looking card grids. Avoid tiny hard-to-read labels, cramped display type, weak contrast, decorative gradient text, excessive glass effects that hurt readability, and animations that feel noisy or block content.

## Design Principles

- Lead with credibility, then personality: every expressive visual choice should support Kehinde's real frontend engineering story.
- Make the work easy to scan: hiring teams and clients should understand experience, stack, projects, impact, and contact paths without fighting the layout.
- Keep the cinematic identity, but make it readable: atmosphere is valuable only when text, contrast, and interaction remain clear.
- Show creative engineering judgment: motion, terminal interactions, day/night mode, and project details should feel intentional rather than gimmicky.
- Stay memorable without becoming template-like: avoid repeated portfolio tropes and let the site feel personal to Kehinde's work and ambitions.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast for body text and key interface states. Preserve readable text sizes on mobile, visible keyboard focus, logical navigation, descriptive link text, and reduced-motion support for entrance animations, pulsing indicators, spinning icons, and smooth scrolling.
